Deep Claim: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026. Developer: One Finger Games.

Deep Claim is a mining game that you play in a web browser or as an Android app. This page explains what data the game uses. Short version: we do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any account, and we do not sell data.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (money, upgrades, the dug mine), sound settings and a few counters (for example the day you first played) are saved only on your device, in the browser or app storage. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app, clearing the browser data or using the in-game "start over" button removes them.

2. Anonymous usage statistics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count how the game is played: page visits and game events such as "first sale", "reached 50 m" or "came back the next day". GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not store your IP address. It receives technical information such as browser or device type, screen size, language, the referring page and an approximate country derived from the IP address at the moment of the request. This data cannot identify you personally and is used only to improve the game.

3. Advertising

The current version shows no real ads. Some buttons offer a bonus "for watching an ad", but they show a placeholder screen only, with no ad network involved and no data shared. If real ads are added later, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and it will name the ad provider and the data it uses.

4. Links to other sites

The game may contain a link to an optional sign-up form (Google Forms) for news about the game. If you open it, Google's privacy policy applies to that form. Filling it in is not required to play.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

6. Permissions

The Android app uses the internet permission only to send the anonymous statistics described above. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.
